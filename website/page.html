How it works

No new editor to learn and nothing to paste. Polish works on the text you already selected.

Step 1

Select your text

Highlight a sentence, a paragraph or a whole email in any app.

Step 2

Press the shortcut

Ctrl Alt G to fix, or Ctrl Alt R to rephrase. A card appears next to your cursor.

Step 3

Replace it

Review the changes and press Enter. The new text goes straight back where it was.

Small tool, sharp edits

Polish makes the smallest change that fixes the problem, so your text still sounds like you.

Grammar and spelling

Every fix is marked in the text and listed with a one-line reason: spelling, grammar, punctuation or style.

Three ways to say it

Rephrase gives you a formal, a concise and a friendly version. Pick one and replace. You can rename the styles.

English and Hebrew

Hebrew stays Hebrew and the card switches to right-to-left. Mixed text keeps each part in its own language.

Instant fix

Ctrl Alt Shift G fixes and replaces without a card. Ctrl Z undoes it.

Your clipboard is kept

Polish copies your selection to read it, then puts back whatever was on your clipboard.

Fast mode

Switch to a smaller model from the tray menu when speed matters more than nuance.

Shortcuts

Change any of them in Settings. On a Mac, Ctrl is Control and Alt is Option.

Where your text goes

Polish has no servers and no account. It talks to one place: Anthropic's API, using your own key.

Text you check is sent to Anthropic

Each check sends the selected text to Claude over an encrypted connection. Don't use Polish on patient-identifiable or other confidential information unless your organization allows it.

Your key stays on your computer

The API key is saved in your user folder (desktop app) or in your browser profile (extension). Polish never sends it anywhere except Anthropic.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license, so you or your IT team can check exactly what it does.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Is Polish free?

The app is free and open source. Checks use your own Anthropic API key, so you pay Anthropic for what you use. A typical paragraph costs a fraction of a cent, and fast mode costs less.

Does it work offline?

No. Claude does the checking in Anthropic's cloud, so you need an internet connection that can reach api.anthropic.com. If a firewall blocks it, Polish shows "Can't reach the Anthropic API".

Which languages does it support?

English and Hebrew are tested, including right-to-left layout. Other languages generally work too, since Claude keeps the language you wrote in.

Does it work in Google Docs?

The Chrome extension can't reach Google Docs, because Docs doesn't use normal text boxes. The desktop app works there: select the text and press the shortcut.

Will it rewrite my text?

Fix makes the smallest edits that correct real errors and leaves your style alone. Rephrase is for when you want a different wording, and you choose whether to use it.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I install it?

The installers aren't code-signed yet, which costs money each year. The full source is on GitHub, and you can build it yourself if you prefer.
