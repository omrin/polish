Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what Polish collects and why. The controller is [operator legal name], [address], [contact email].

The text you check

When you run a check, the text you selected is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to Anthropic PBC (USA), whose Claude model produces the result. We don't store the text or the result. Our logs record only metadata: time, size of the request in tokens, credits used, and errors. Logs are deleted after 7 days. Anthropic processes the text under its commercial terms and may keep it for up to 30 days for safety and abuse monitoring; it doesn't use it to train models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Don't submit patient-identifiable or other confidential information unless your organization permits it. Polish is not a HIPAA-covered service and we don't sign business associate agreements.

Your account

We keep your email address, your Google account ID if you sign in with Google, your credit balance and its history (purchases, refunds, and per-check credit charges with token counts), and a list of signed-in devices with their names and last-used dates. Sign-in codes and tokens are stored only as one-way hashes.

Payments

Paddle.com is our Merchant of Record. It collects your payment details and billing information directly; we never see your card number. See Paddle's privacy policy.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting and database), Anthropic (AI processing), Resend (sign-in emails), Google (if you choose Google sign-in), Paddle (payments). Some are in the USA; transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Cookies

The website sets one cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Paddle's checkout sets its own cookies when you buy credits.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your email and Google ID and sign out every device. We keep anonymized purchase records for [7] years where tax law requires it. IP addresses are used only, in hashed form, for rate limiting and are discarded within 2 days.

Your rights

You can see your balance and history on the Account page, delete your account there, or contact us at [contact email] to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to processing. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority).
