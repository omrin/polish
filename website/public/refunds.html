Refund Policy

Last updated: [date]

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. To ask for a refund, contact Paddle through the link in your receipt email, or email us at [support email].

Credit packs

  • You can get a full refund for a pack within 14 days of purchase if you haven't used any credits from it.
  • If you've used some of it, we may refund the unused part at our discretion.
  • Free credits (such as welcome credits) can't be refunded.

When a refund is issued, the refunded credits are removed from your balance. If that takes your balance below zero, you'll need to top up before running more checks.

Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback instead of asking for a refund, we may suspend your account while it's reviewed.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any right you have under the consumer protection law that applies to you.
